01 / PROFILE

About me

I am a public health researcher and research methodologist specializing in epidemiology, biostatistics, and population-based health research. My work focuses on generating rigorous scientific evidence to address health inequalities, strengthen health systems, and inform evidence-based policies. As Director of the Ogaansho Research and Consultancy Center (ORCC), I am committed to advancing methodological excellence, interdisciplinary research collaboration, and research capacity development, with a growing interest in health informatics and applied data science.
